Chef-Cook Process

Before, During and After the Race

1Schedule and Contact

  • Each chef’s schedule, partner and nutritionist are known at the beginning of the year
  • The nutritionist must contact the chef

2Menus and Approval

S-3

Recommendations

The nutritionist sends the chef the race recommendations, completed in the Excel spreadsheet.

This cannot be done earlier: it depends on the team strategy, from which the nutrition strategy follows.

+1
week

Cross-checking

The chef cross-checks the recommendations against the proposed menus.

  • Follow low-fibre requirements and other recommendations…
S-2

Sending the menus

The chef sends the menus to the nutritionist, No later than 2 weeks before the race starts.

D-10

Approval

The nutritionist responds promptly. The menus are approved No later than 10 days before the race starts.

3Orders and Hotels

Orders can be placed once the menus have been approved.

  • Stores: Metro, Biocoop, Super U
  • A list helps ensure similar products are available for every race, with the relevant store numbers
  • Use as many organic products as possible

Email to the hotel

Send the order and/or a sheet listing what the hotel needs to provide for the buffet.

CC the relevant people: Chef 2 or the nutrition assistant.

4Preparation and Arrival

  • 1 preparation day before the race
  • Grand Tour: 2 full preparation days before departure
  • Arrive 24 hours before the first rider
  • Plan carefully for the riders’ arrival and arrival time

5WhatsApp Group and Dinner

Two days before the event, create a group with the nutrition team, riders and bus driver.

Dinner on arrival

Ask whether dinner is needed, depending on the arrival time.

  • If they arrive late, it may make sense for them not to eat.
  • Sometimes they prefer to eat a little later, with good-quality food
  • Or have a snack before bed

6Snacks

Schedule

  • On the first day, the chef shares in the group the spreadsheet of snack choices for the whole week (repeat this on each rest day during a Grand Tour)
  • Plan snacks ahead
  • Each day before 8 p.m., the chef receives the weights for the next day’s snacks
  • Some nutritionists plan a week at a time and send all the weights together

Recipes

  • Offer bowls: 7 in total (the number is expected to increase)
  • Only the quantities of meat and starch vary
  • Also offer a plain bowl (starch + protein) and a handheld option
  • Sweet options: the standard snack and rice pudding
  • The complete recipe list is shared with riders in advance; they receive all snack recipes at the beginning of the season

Packing into containers

The nutrition assistant or Chef 2 helps pack the snacks.

Each container must have a label:

  • rider’s name
  • date
  • snack name
  • weights of ingredients that vary

Sports cake

  • Prepare at the same time as the snack

Metro containers to purchase

Metro Professional snack-container references, sold in packs of 50. Bowl sauces and rice-pudding toppings should be served in sauce cups.

7Race-Day Setup

Buffet

Set up the buffet and make sure everything is in place.

Buffet equipment (see photos): coffee machine, warming plate, toaster, bread knife, bread board, cooler.

Menu Boards and Hexis

  • Set up the menu boards and use the exact name of each recipe listed in Hexis (ensure the app is updated with the correct names)
  • If anything changes, always show the packaging to the athletes: they can scan it and adjust the quantity according to their intake

Riders’ Table

  • Still and sparkling water
  • Butter
  • Salted butter biscuits
  • Bread
  • Items in the breakfast crate
  • Parmesan

8Breakfast

Morning buffet including:

  • rice pudding or porridge
  • berries
  • crêpes, pancakes and waffles
  • made-to-order omelettes (plain, cheese, ham)
  • the various items from the breakfast crate

9During and After the Race

During the race

Clean the truck daily, inside and out.

The truck reflects the team’s image.

At the end

  • Clean the truck thoroughly
  • Refill fuel, gas and AdBlue
  • Coordinate with the other chef
  • Report any lost equipment so it can be replaced in time; it’s not a problem to flag it.
